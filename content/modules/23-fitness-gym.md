# Fitness & Gym

**Headline:** Members, memberships, check-in, classes and personal training in one gym system.
**Tagline:** Gym and fitness-club management software for the UAE and GCC. Works on its own.

## Overview
Run a gym or fitness club from one system: sell and renew memberships, check members in at the front desk, fill classes, schedule personal training and take payments. It connects to Finance, CRM and the other Vrodux modules when a business uses them, and it works on its own with no other module required.

## Who it's for
Gyms, fitness studios, health clubs, personal-training studios and multi-branch fitness groups in the UAE and GCC.

## Features
- **Members** — profiles with photo, emergency contact and waiver status; member numbers; freeze and renew; blacklist.
- **Memberships and plans** — gym, classes, combo, personal training and day-pass plans; joining fees; class credits; visit limits; freeze rules; guest passes; off-peak hours. A membership keeps the terms it was sold under, so later price changes never alter existing members.
- **Front desk check-in** — scan or type a member number. It refuses expired, frozen, blacklisted or unpaid members, and members with an unsigned waiver (configurable). Staff can override with permission. Every scan is logged, and a live "who's inside" view shows occupancy.
- **Classes** — class types and timetable, online and desk booking, a waitlist with automatic promotion, late-cancel rules and class credits.
- **Personal training** — trainers, session packages, booking without clashes, and completed and no-show tracking.
- **Payments** — receipts, part payments and balances, void with a reason, and optional posting to Finance.
- **Workout and diet programs** — reusable templates, assigned to each member.
- **Body measurements** — tracked over time.
- **Facility** — equipment with maintenance logs, and locker assignment.
- **Leads** — a sales pipeline for trial and enquiry leads.
- **Member portal** — members sign in with member number and PIN to see their membership, balance, payments and visits, and to book or cancel classes. The gym switches it on.
- **Multi-branch** — one gym group, several branches.
- **Reports and expiry alerts** — nine reports in the Reports hub, and a daily alert for memberships about to expire.
- **Vrodux AI assistant** — ask in plain language for members, classes, payments and check-ins. It can take a payment, check someone in or book a class, always after you confirm.
- **Roles and permissions** — permissions per area for front desk, trainers and managers.

## Works with
Finance (optional posting of payments), CRM, Reports hub, HR. Fitness & Gym works on its own and does not need any other module.

## Access control
Role-based permissions per area, so front desk, trainers and managers each see and do only what they should.

## Do not claim yet
- Turnstile or door hardware.
- Online card payments for members, native payment gateways.
- Arabic screens for this module (English only for now).
- Wearable or app integrations, and a member mobile app.
