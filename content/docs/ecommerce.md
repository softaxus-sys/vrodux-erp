@@ open-your-online-store | How to open your online store | One store per workspace, on a web address you choose or your own domain, kept private until you launch.
E-commerce is your own online store, **built on Inventory**. Products, prices and stock come straight from Inventory, so nothing is entered twice.

## Your store address

Each workspace has **one store**. Choose a web address for it, or use **your own domain**, which you set up with DNS.

## Preview before you launch

Your store stays in a **private preview** until you launch it. Use it to check your pages, products and checkout first.

## Before you start

Put the products you want to sell in Inventory first. See [Publish products from Inventory](/docs/ecommerce/publish-products-from-inventory).

@@ settings-payments-and-delivery | How to set up payments and delivery | Choose how customers pay, then set delivery zones, rates, a free-delivery threshold and tax.
## Payments

Customers can pay in three ways:

- **Card**, through your own **Stripe** or **PayPal** account
- **Cash on delivery**
- **Bank transfer**

## Delivery

- Create **delivery zones** and a **rate** for each.
- Set a **free-delivery threshold**.
- Set **tax**.

## Discount codes

Create **discount codes** for your customers to use at checkout.

## Totals are worked out by the server

Totals, stock and prices are always worked out by the server, never trusted from the customer's browser.

@@ design-pages-and-collections | How to design pages and collections | Build pages from sections with a live preview, then add menus, collections and discount codes.
## The page builder

Pages are built from **sections**: hero, product grid, text, images and call to action. A **live preview** shows the page as you build it.

## Menus and collections

- Add **menus** so customers can find their way around.
- Group products into **collections**.

## Launch

When you are happy with the private preview, launch the store.

@@ publish-products-from-inventory | How to publish products from Inventory | List stock online as drafts or published, in one click, and keep photos with the stock item.
## Online listings

A **listing** is what customers see: name, description, photos, options such as size and colour (up to three), and search details. Each variant is an ordinary Inventory product with its own SKU, barcode, price and stock, so POS, purchasing and reports stay correct.

## List stock in one click

1. Open the stock list, or the **Not yet listed** worklist.
2. Select the items you want to sell online.
3. List them as **drafts** or **published**.

Photos stay with the stock item. See [Online listings and photos](/docs/inventory/online-listings-and-photos) and [Import stock with pictures](/docs/inventory/import-stock-with-pictures).

@@ connect-shopify | How to connect Shopify | Publish products from Inventory to a Shopify store, keep stock in step and bring its orders into Vrodux.
## What the connection does

Connect a Shopify store to:

- **Publish products** from Inventory
- Keep **stock in step both ways**
- Bring its **orders** into Vrodux

@@ manage-store-orders | How to manage store orders | Stock is reserved when an order is placed. Mark it paid, pack, ship, deliver or refund.
## Stock and orders

**Stock is reserved** when the order is placed and **released** if it is cancelled or never paid.

## Move an order along

Mark it **paid**, then **pack**, **ship**, **deliver** or **refund**. Customers are **emailed at each step**.

## Customer accounts

Customers can check out with or without an account. Those with an account sign in to the store, with password reset by email.
