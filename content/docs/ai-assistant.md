## Where it works

Chat with Vrodux AI in a **side panel** or on a **full page**. Replies are formatted, with tables and lists. Open it from the **Ask Vrodux** button.

## Across modules

The picker lists a **specialist for each module you use**: CRM, Sales, Purchase, Finance, HR, Inventory, POS, Restaurant, Hospitality, Real Estate, Library, Lead Generation, SEO, E-commerce, Visa, Trade, Projects, Manufacturing, Construction, Recipe, B2B, Education, Healthcare and Insurance. **Auto mode** picks the right one. It finds the right tools when it needs them, from **more than 300 tools** across 20+ modules, to list, look up, create and change records and to run steps such as approvals and status changes. Examples: log a bug in a project, create a quotation, check a guest in, issue a library book, approve a purchase requisition, change an SEO fix status.

## Every change is confirmed

Anything that **creates or changes** data is held for your confirmation. You see a **field-by-field summary** and choose to confirm or reject.

## Safeguards

- It respects the **signed-in user's permissions** and your enabled modules.
- There are **no delete tools**.
- It cannot make, void or refund a **POS sale** from chat.
- You can connect **your own AI provider key**, or use the shared allowance included with your workspace (a monthly message limit applies).
- **Voice**: push-to-talk in the chat, with spoken replies where voice is enabled for your workspace.

Answers can be wrong, so check the summary before you confirm.
