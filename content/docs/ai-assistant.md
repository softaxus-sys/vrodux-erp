## Where it works

Chat with the assistant in a **side panel** or on a **full page**. Replies are formatted, with tables and lists.

## Across modules

In **auto mode** it works across all your modules. Name an agent such as CRM, Finance, HR, Restaurant, Visa or Trade to focus it. It finds the right tools when it needs them, from **more than 250 tools** across the modules, to list, look up, create and change records. Examples: log a bug in a project, raise a purchase requisition, create a quotation, add a shipment cost.

## Every change is confirmed

Anything that **creates or changes** data is held for your confirmation. You see a **field-by-field summary** and choose to confirm or reject.

## Safeguards

- It respects the **signed-in user's permissions** and your enabled modules.
- There are **no delete tools**.
- It cannot make a **POS sale, void or refund** from chat.
- You **bring your own AI provider key**.

Answers can be wrong, so check the summary before you confirm.
