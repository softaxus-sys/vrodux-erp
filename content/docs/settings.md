## Two-factor authentication

Two-factor authentication uses an **authenticator app** and gives you **backup codes**. It is optional for each user, and you can **enforce it for the whole workspace**.

## Security settings

These settings are actually enforced:

- **password** length, complexity and expiry
- **maximum login attempts**
- **session timeout**
- **single session** per user
- an **IP whitelist**

## Your devices

You can see your sessions and devices and sign out any of them.

## Users and email

Users created by an administrator **verify their email**. See [Users, Roles, Teams & Permissions](/docs/start-here/users-roles-teams-permissions).

@@ plans-trial-and-billing | How plans, the free trial and billing work | The 14-day trial, how plans are sized by users, and how to subscribe.
## The free trial

Every workspace starts with a **14-day free trial with unlimited users**. Reminder emails arrive **7, 3 and 1 days** before it ends.

## Plans

After the trial, the only thing that changes between plans is the **number of users**: 3, 10 or 50, and Enterprise through sales. **Every module is available on every plan.**

## Subscribing

Subscribe by card through **Stripe** or **PayPal**, and manage it in the billing portal. Checkout does not let you choose a plan smaller than your active user count. Access is restored the moment payment goes through, and **data is never deleted** if a subscription lapses.

See [Your plan](/docs/settings/users-security-plan) and [pricing](/pricing).

@@ notifications-and-apps | Notifications, mobile app and desktop app | Real-time alerts, installing the web app, the mobile app and the Windows till app.
## Notifications

Alerts appear in real time as a **bell** and a toast, with optional sound and desktop notifications. They are **stored**, so you can read them later. They cover things like CRM assignments, leave and payroll approvals, purchase approvals, project assignments, rent and vacancy alerts, trade alerts and quotation responses.

## Web, phone and till

- The **web app** can be **installed to your home screen** and works on phones and tablets.
- The **mobile app** covers sign-in with two-factor, CRM, HR self-service, approvals and more. See [What's on your phone](/docs/mobile-app/whats-on-your-phone).
- The **Windows desktop app** is for tills. The installer asks for your server address.

## Languages and currency

The main app supports **English and Arabic**. Set your company currency once. Exchange rates use a USD base and refresh daily.
