@@ b2b-proposals-contracts-and-tickets | B2B: proposals, contracts and support tickets | Track proposals, contracts and support tickets for a professional services business.
The B2B pack has a **summary** tab and three working lists that sit alongside CRM.

- **Proposals:** create them and update their status.
- **Contracts:** keep your contracts and update their status.
- **Support tickets:** record and update tickets.

You can create, update status and delete in each list. Each has view, create, edit and delete permissions, customised per role.

@@ education-admissions-students-and-enrollments | Education: admissions, students and enrollments | Track admissions, keep student records and enroll students.
The Education pack has a **summary** tab and three lists.

- **Admissions**
- **Students**
- **Enrollments:** enroll a student and update the status.

Each list has its own view, create, edit and delete permissions.

@@ healthcare-patients-appointments-and-treatment-plans | Healthcare: patients, appointments and treatment plans | Keep a patient list, book and complete appointments, and track treatment plans.
The Healthcare pack has a **summary** tab and three lists.

- **Patients**
- **Appointments:** book them and mark them **complete**.
- **Treatment plans:** track them and update their status.

This is a lightweight clinic workflow. It is not a full clinical record system. Each list has its own permissions.

@@ insurance-policies-renewals-and-claims | Insurance: policies, renewals and claims | Manage policies, renew them and record payments, and resolve claims.
The Insurance pack has a **summary** tab and three lists.

- **Policies**
- **Renewals:** **renew** a policy and **record payments**.
- **Claims:** **resolve** or **approve** claims. Approval is controlled by its own **approve** permission.

Each list has view, create, edit and delete permissions, customised per role.
