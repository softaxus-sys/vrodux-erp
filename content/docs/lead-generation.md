Lead Generation is live for the **UAE**.

## Find businesses

1. Open Lead Generation and search by **category** and **area**. Results come from real businesses, with website information added.
2. **Finding is free.**

## Reveal a contact

Spend **credits** to reveal a contact. If the contact turns out to be unusable or bounces, the credit is **refunded**. Credit packs are bought by Stripe or PayPal.

## Manage prospects

Your prospects list can be edited, imported and exported, and you can **push prospects to your CRM**.

## Connect your mailbox

Campaigns send from **your own mailbox**: Google, Microsoft or SMTP/IMAP.

## Build the campaign

1. Create a **campaign** with several steps, each a template.
2. Set the **send window** in your time zone. Warm-up limits keep sending volume sensible.
3. Choose your prospects.

Every email carries an **unsubscribe link** and your sender address. **Suppression lists** keep unsubscribed people out, and a campaign **pauses itself** on bounces or complaints.

## Read replies

Replies arrive in the **Inbox**, classified so you can see which ones matter.

## Business profile

In settings, your business profile is pre-filled from your company settings and industry: what you offer, your ideal customers and preferred business types.

## Good to know

Cold email must comply with local law and recipients' consent expectations. Connecting a Gmail account for the public requires Google's app verification.
