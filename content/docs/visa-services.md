## Create a case

1. Open Visa Services and start the **case wizard**.
2. Choose the **visa type**, the **client** and the **applicants**, including dependants.
3. The case appears on the **board** and in the **list**, with an **SLA due date** and an **overdue** chip when it slips.

## Status path

A case moves **draft → documents pending → documents complete → submitted → in review → approved → issued → closed**. There are also rework and request-for-information loops. Only the **legal next steps** are offered.

## Document checklist

Each applicant gets a **checklist copied from the visa type**. When the last document is **verified**, the case advances.

## Link to CRM

Link a case to a **CRM account**, or create a case from a **CRM lead**.

## Visa types

A **UAE catalogue** (employment new, renewal and cancellation, family, visit, golden, freelance and student) is copied into each workspace. Edit **fees**, **processing days** and **required documents**.

## Renewals

Vrodux tracks **passport**, **document** and **issued-visa expiry** with **30, 60, 90 and 180-day** horizons, so you know who to contact before a visa lapses.

## Billing

From a case, **generate a draft invoice in Finance**: service fee plus government fee. The case links back to the invoice.

## Submissions

Record **government transactions** per case. Recording is **manual** today, and the channel settings page stores credentials encrypted. Vrodux does not connect live to government systems.

## Dashboard

The dashboard shows totals, overdue cases, expiring visas, cases by status and type, revenue and **PRO workload**.
