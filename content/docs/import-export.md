Import & Export works on its own and is available automatically for tenants whose industry is Import & Export.

## Create the shipment

1. Create a shipment and choose **import** or **export**.
2. Add the **goods lines**: HS code, quantity, weight and volume.
3. Add **containers**.
4. Link a **Purchase** or **Sales** order if you want its lines copied in.

You can also **import goods lines** from Excel or CSV.

## Move it along

Update the status as the cargo moves: **booked → in transit → arrived → customs → cleared → delivered → closed**. The milestone history is kept.

## Parties

Keep your **suppliers**, **buyers**, **forwarders**, **clearing agents**, **carriers** and **banks** as parties and attach them to shipments.

@@ trade-documents-costs-and-landed-cost | How to manage documents, costs and landed cost | Attach trade documents, record costs, and share them across goods to see true landed cost.
## Documents

Attach files up to 8 MB. Expiry dates are watched for things like **L/C**, **permits** and **certificates**. You can generate a **commercial invoice** and **packing list** PDF.

## Costs

Record **freight**, **insurance**, **duty** and other costs. They are shared across goods lines by **value**, **weight**, **volume** or **quantity** and converted to your currency, which gives the **landed cost** of each line.

Costs and payments have **separate permissions**, so staff can track cargo without seeing what it cost.

@@ duty-payments-and-alerts | How duty estimates, payments and alerts work | Estimate duty by HS code, schedule payments, and get alerts for late cargo and expiring documents.
## Duty estimate

Add **duty rates per HS code**. The longest matching code wins. The **duty estimate** shows its working, and any line without a matching rate is **flagged**, never treated as duty-free.

## Payments

Record **advance**, **balance**, **L/C** and **TT** payments with due dates.

## Alerts and dashboard

The dashboard and daily in-app alerts warn about **late ETAs**, **port free days used up** (demurrage), **expiring documents**, and **payments due or overdue**.

## Stock in transit and reports

Inventory shows **incoming and outgoing quantity** per product. There are **8 reports** in the Reports hub.
