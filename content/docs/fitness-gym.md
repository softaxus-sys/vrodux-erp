@@ getting-started-with-fitness-and-gym | Getting started with Fitness & Gym | Create a branch, set up your plans and add your first member.
Fitness & Gym works on its own and does not need other modules. If you also use Finance or CRM, it connects to them.

## Create a branch

1. Open **Fitness & Gym** and go to settings.
2. Add your first **branch**. If you run a gym group, add each branch the same way.

[screenshot: The branch list in Fitness & Gym settings]

## Set up your plans

1. Open **Plans** and add a plan. You can create gym, classes, combo, personal training and day-pass plans.
2. Set the price and the **joining fee**.
3. Add the rules that apply: **class credits**, **visit limits**, **freeze rules**, **guest passes** and **off-peak hours**.

[screenshot: A membership plan with its price, joining fee and rules]

## Add your first member

1. Open **Members** and choose to add a member.
2. Enter the name, contact details, **emergency contact** and a **photo**.
3. Record whether the **waiver** is signed.
4. Save. The member gets a **member number**.

[screenshot: A new member profile]

Next, [sell their first membership](/docs/fitness-gym/selling-and-renewing-memberships).

@@ selling-and-renewing-memberships | Selling and renewing memberships | Sell a plan, renew it, freeze it, and understand why later price changes never alter existing members.
## Sell a membership

1. Open the member and choose **Sell membership**.
2. Pick a plan and a start date.
3. Take the payment, or record a part payment and keep a balance. See [taking payments](/docs/fitness-gym/taking-payments).

[screenshot: Selling a membership to a member]

## The membership keeps its terms

A membership keeps the terms it was sold under. If you change a plan's price or rules later, **existing members are not affected**. Only new sales use the new terms.

## Renew

Open the membership and choose **Renew**. A daily alert lists memberships that are about to expire so the front desk can follow up.

## Freeze

If a member needs to pause, **freeze** the membership within the plan's freeze rules. A frozen member is refused at check-in until the freeze ends.

## Blacklist

You can **blacklist** a member. They are refused at check-in until you remove the blacklist.

@@ front-desk-check-in | Front desk check-in | Scan or type a member number, see why a member is refused, override with permission and watch who is inside.
## Check a member in

1. Open **Check-in** at the front desk.
2. **Scan** the member number, or type it.
3. The member is let in, and the visit is logged.

[screenshot: The front desk check-in screen]

## When a member is refused

Check-in **refuses** a member who is:

- expired
- frozen
- blacklisted
- unpaid
- missing a signed waiver (this check is configurable)

The screen shows the reason, so the front desk can explain it.

## Override

A staff member with the right permission can **override** a refusal. Overrides are not available to everyone, so give this permission only to the roles that need it.

## Every scan is logged

Every scan, allowed or refused, is recorded. The **who's inside** view shows current occupancy live.

[screenshot: The who's inside view]

@@ classes-and-waitlists | Classes and waitlists | Set up class types and a timetable, take bookings, and let the waitlist fill empty places automatically.
## Class types and timetable

1. Open **Classes** and add a **class type**, such as yoga or spinning.
2. Add the class to the **timetable** with a day, time, trainer and capacity.

[screenshot: The class timetable]

## Bookings

Staff can book a member at the desk. Members can book online through the [member portal](/docs/fitness-gym/member-portal-setup). Booking uses a **class credit** when the plan has credits.

## Waitlist

When a class is full, the member joins the **waitlist**. If someone cancels, the next person on the waitlist is **promoted automatically**.

## Late cancellation

Set **late-cancel rules** for bookings that are cancelled too close to the start of a class.

@@ personal-training | Personal training | Add trainers, sell session packages, book sessions without clashes and track completed and no-show sessions.
## Trainers

Add your trainers and the days they work.

## Session packages

Create a **session package**, for example ten sessions, and sell it to a member like any other plan.

## Book a session

1. Open **Personal training** and choose to book a session.
2. Pick the member, the trainer and a time.
3. The booking stops you double-booking a trainer.

[screenshot: Booking a personal training session]

## Completed and no-shows

After the session, mark it **completed** or **no-show**, so you can see what was delivered.

@@ taking-payments | Taking payments | Take full or part payments, track balances, void with a reason and optionally post to Finance.
## Take a payment

1. Open the member and choose **Take payment**.
2. Enter the amount and how it was paid.
3. A **receipt** is created.

[screenshot: Taking a payment and the receipt]

## Part payments and balances

A member can pay in parts. The **balance** stays on the membership until it is paid.

## Void a payment

If a payment was entered by mistake, **void** it. You must give a **reason**, which is kept on the record.

## Post to Finance

If you use Finance, you can switch on **posting to Finance** so payments reach the ledger. It is optional. Fitness & Gym works without Finance.

@@ programs-and-measurements | Programs and measurements | Build reusable workout and diet programs, assign them to members and track body measurements over time.
## Programs

1. Open **Programs** and create a **workout** or **diet** template.
2. Reuse the template for any number of members.

[screenshot: A workout program template]

## Assign a program

Open the member and **assign** a program. Each member can have their own.

## Body measurements

Record a member's **body measurements** on each visit that matters. The member profile shows how they **change over time**.

[screenshot: A member's body measurements over time]

@@ member-portal-setup | Member portal setup | Switch on the member portal so members can see their membership and book classes.
The member portal is **off until you switch it on**.

## Switch it on

1. Open Fitness & Gym settings.
2. Turn on the **member portal** for your gym.

## What members can do

Members sign in with their **member number and PIN**. They can see:

- their membership and its balance
- their payments
- their visits

They can also **book or cancel classes**.

[screenshot: The member portal after sign-in]

The member portal is in English for now.

@@ roles-and-permissions | Roles and permissions | Give front desk, trainers and managers access to the right areas only.
Permissions are set **per area**, so each role sees and does only what it needs.

## A common way to set it up

- **Front desk:** members, check-in, class bookings and payments.
- **Trainers:** classes, personal training, programs and measurements.
- **Managers:** everything, including reports.

## Override permission

Staff can override a refused check-in only if they have permission. Give it to the roles that should be able to let a refused member in.

[screenshot: Role permissions for Fitness & Gym]

@@ reports | Reports | Use the nine Fitness & Gym reports and the daily expiry alert.
Fitness & Gym adds **nine reports** to the Reports hub. The hub shows only the modules and reports you have access to, and you can export to CSV or PDF.

[screenshot: The Fitness & Gym reports in the Reports hub]

## Expiry alerts

A **daily alert** lists memberships about to expire, so your team can contact those members before they lapse.

## Ask the assistant

You can also ask the Vrodux AI assistant in plain language about members, classes, payments and check-ins. It can take a payment, check someone in or book a class, and always asks you to confirm first.
