@@ create-and-send-a-quotation | How to create and send a quotation | Build a branded quotation from a template and let the customer accept or decline it online.
A quotation is the first step of selling in Vrodux. It looks like a proper business document and the customer can answer it from a link.

## Create a quotation

1. Open Sales and create a new quotation, or start one from a CRM lead or account.
2. Pick a template. A default "Standard Quotation" exists for every workspace, and you can save your own boilerplate and prefilled lines.
3. Fill in the title, reference, cover note, sections and line items (with units and notes).
4. Add optional extras. They are shown to the customer but not charged.
5. Set the header discount, tax, payment terms, terms and conditions, prepared-by and validity.
6. Add any **custom fields** you need, such as project code, lead time or warranty. No setup is required.

The letterhead comes from your company settings: logo, name, address, TRN, signature, stamp and accent colour.

## Send it

- Email it with the link, or
- copy the link and share it, or
- print or download it as a PDF.

The customer opens the link without logging in and can accept or decline online. You can see **sent, opened and answered** with dates, and the creator and the lead owner are alerted.

## After sending

- **Duplicate as a new revision** to change something and send again.
- **Convert to a sales order** once accepted.
- **Create or link a Finance invoice** (needs Finance).

## Good to know

- Editing a template never changes quotations that were already sent.
- Deleting a quotation revokes its customer link. This is intentional.

@@ sales-orders-delivery-and-returns | How to handle sales orders, delivery and returns | Confirm an order, record delivery challans against it, and approve or reject returns.
## Sales orders

Create a sales order by converting a quotation, then confirm it. You can track its status and delete drafts.

## Deliver the order

Record each delivery with a **delivery challan** against the order. The order moves to **shipped** and then **delivered** as the delivered quantities add up, so a partial delivery is handled correctly.

## Returns

Customers can return goods. A sales return is approved or rejected by someone with the approve permission. Approved returns can be posted to Finance through the Finance posting queue.

## Related

- Customers: the customer master used by sales documents.
- Production: use "Plan production" on a sales order if you use the Manufacturing module.
- Invoicing: see [Invoicing, Expenses & the Ledger](/docs/finance/invoicing-expenses-ledger).

Permissions cover quotations, orders and returns separately, each with view, create, edit, delete and approve.
