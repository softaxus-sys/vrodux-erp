@@ capture-and-import-leads | How to capture and import leads | Bring leads into CRM from ads, forms, portals, a spreadsheet or by hand, without creating duplicates.
Every lead in CRM starts somewhere: an ad, a website form, a property portal, a calendar booking, or a spreadsheet someone sent you. CRM accepts all of them and applies the same duplicate rules and assignment routing to every source.

## Ways leads arrive

- **Meta lead ads** from Facebook and Instagram.
- **Website forms.** CRM hosts a form you can embed on your own site.
- **Custom API and webhooks**, plus Zapier and Make for anything else you already use.
- **Google Forms and Sheets**, through a small script.
- **Calendly** bookings.
- **Property Finder** and **Bayut/Dubizzle**, for property businesses.
- **CSV or Excel import**, for lists you already have.
- **Manual entry**, one lead at a time.

## Connect a lead source

1. Open the CRM integrations area.
2. Choose the source you want to connect.
3. Follow the built-in setup guide shown for that source.
4. Send a test lead and check that it appears in the lead list.

If a portal feed stops working, administrators receive an email and an in-app alert, and another when it recovers.

## Import leads from Excel or CSV

1. Open the lead list and choose Import.
2. Upload your file. CRM matches your columns to lead fields automatically; check the matches and correct any that are wrong.
3. Review the duplicate warnings. CRM detects duplicates so the same person is not created twice.
4. Confirm the import.

## What gets captured

Each lead can hold WhatsApp number, budget, what they are interested in, their message, and a planned purchase timeframe. Leads from ads also keep their marketing attribution (platform, campaign, ad and form) and any form responses, so you can see later which source produced revenue.

## Good to know

- Budget written as text, such as "50k–100k", "1.5M", "5 lakh" or "2 crore", is understood. A numeric value is only worked out when it can be trusted.
- Assignment of integration-captured leads is done by administrators; reassign a lead from the lead list when you need to.

See [CRM: Leads, Pipeline & Customers](/docs/crm/leads-pipeline-customers) for how these leads connect to the rest of CRM.

@@ lead-scoring-hot-warm-cold | How lead scoring works | Understand the 0–100 lead score and the Hot, Warm and Cold bands, and how to use them to decide who to call first.
Every lead is scored automatically so your team can see who to call first.

## What the score is based on

The score runs from 0 to 100 and takes into account:

- how contactable the lead is
- how urgently they want to buy
- their budget
- intent keywords in what they wrote
- the quality of the source they came from
- the priority you set
- how engaged they have been

## Hot, Warm and Cold

The score is turned into a band: **Hot**, **Warm** or **Cold**. The lead list can be sorted hottest first, so the first rows are the people most worth calling.

## How to use it

1. Open the lead list.
2. Sort by score so the hottest leads are at the top.
3. Work through the Hot leads first, logging each call on the lead's timeline.
4. Review Warm leads next, and leave Cold leads for follow-up sequences.

## Good to know

- The score updates as the lead record changes, for example when you add a budget or a timeframe.
- A better-filled lead scores higher, so ask your team to complete the contact details, budget and timeframe fields.

@@ work-the-pipeline-and-convert-leads | How to work the pipeline and convert leads | Move deals through pipeline stages, convert a lead into an account, contact and opportunity, and record wins and losses.
## Move a lead through its statuses

The lead board has columns for new, contacted, qualified and converted. Drag a lead card from one column to the next as you work it, or use the list view with status filters and search.

## Convert a lead

1. Open the lead.
2. Choose Convert.
3. CRM creates an **account**, a **contact** and an **opportunity** in one step.

From here the deal lives in the pipeline.

## Work the pipeline

- Use the pipeline **board** or **list**. Drag a deal between stages as it progresses.
- Each opportunity has a **forecast category**: pipeline, best case, commit or closed. Weighted value is calculated from it.
- Link the opportunity to an account, and add several contacts to the deal with a buying role such as decision maker or champion.
- When a deal is lost, record the **loss reason**. It feeds the Win/Loss report.

## Log your activity

Add calls, meetings, emails, notes and tasks to the lead, opportunity or account. Give a task to a teammate and they receive a notification. Everything appears on one timeline; an account's timeline rolls up its deals' and leads' activity.

## Attach documents

Attach files to leads, opportunities and accounts so the whole team can find them.

@@ control-who-sees-what-in-crm | How to control who sees what in CRM | Use everyone, team and assigned-only access, assign and reassign leads, and file records to a team.
CRM lets you decide how much of the pipeline each person can see.

## The three access levels

For each area (leads, pipeline, accounts and so on) a role can be set to:

- **Everyone:** sees all records.
- **Team:** a team lead sees their team's records.
- **Assigned only:** a rep sees only their own records.

Roles are customised in Settings → Roles & Permissions. See [Users, Roles, Teams & Permissions](/docs/start-here/users-roles-teams-permissions).

## Assign and reassign leads

1. Select one or more leads.
2. Choose Assign and pick a person from the picker, which is grouped by team.
3. Confirm. Every handover is recorded, and the supervisor of the team member who receives the work is notified.

## File records to a team

Use the bulk "file to team" action on leads, opportunities and accounts to move many records to a team at once.

## Separate permissions

Leads, pipeline, customers, reports (view and export) and integrations each have their own permission, so you can, for example, let someone see reports but not change integrations.

@@ send-quotations-from-crm | How to send a quotation from CRM | Create a branded quotation for a lead or account, send it and track the customer's answer. Needs the Sales module.
Quotations are created in CRM using the Sales module's templates. You need access to both.

## Create and send

1. Open the lead or account.
2. Choose to create a quotation and pick a Sales template.
3. Fill in the line items, terms and validity, and preview the branded document.
4. Send it by email, copy the **customer link**, or download it as a PDF.

## What the customer sees

The customer link shows the quotation without logging in. The customer can accept or decline it online. You see when it was sent, opened and answered, and accepted or declined alerts reach the person who created it and the lead owner.

## After acceptance

Quotes follow a converted lead onto its new account. Continue in Sales to convert the accepted quotation into a sales order. See [How to create and send a quotation](/docs/sales/create-and-send-a-quotation).

@@ crm-reports | How to use CRM reports | Eight built-in reports for pipeline, wins and losses, team performance, lead sources, conversion, velocity, activities and account revenue.
CRM has eight built-in reports, available from the Reports hub.

## The reports

- **Sales pipeline**
- **Win/Loss**
- **Team performance**, grouped by team
- **Lead source ROI**
- **Lead conversion**
- **Sales velocity**
- **Activities**
- **Account revenue**

## How to run one

1. Open the Reports hub and choose CRM.
2. Pick a report.
3. Filter by date range, owner or stage.
4. Export the result as CSV or PDF if you need to share it.

## Good to know

- Results respect each person's access tier, so a rep sees only their own numbers.
- Stage history for reports builds up from the date the feature was deployed, so older closed deals use approximate dates.

See [Reports hub](/docs/reports/overview) for how the hub works across modules.
