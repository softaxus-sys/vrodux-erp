Library Management works on its own and does not need other modules.

## Titles and copies

A **title** holds the ISBN, authors, category, type and replacement cost. Each physical **copy** has its own **barcode**, **branch**, **shelf** and **condition**.

## Import

**Bulk import** titles and copies from Excel or CSV. It is safe to run again.

## Print labels and cards

Print **barcode labels** on A4 sheets or label-printer rolls (Code 128), and print **member cards**.

@@ circulation-desk-issue-return-and-reserve | How to issue, return, renew and reserve | Use the circulation desk with a barcode scanner, and handle reservations and branch transfers.
## Issue and return

1. Open the **circulation desk**.
2. Scan the member card, then scan the book barcode to **issue** it.
3. To return, scan the book. You can also **renew** or mark an item **lost**.

## Reservations

Members can reserve a title. There is a **queue per title**, and when a copy comes back it is **held for the next member for 3 days**.

## Transfers between branches

Move a copy between branches. It is **in transit** until the receiving branch marks it **received**.

Staff can be limited to their own branch or branches. Managers are never limited.

@@ members-fines-and-reminders | How to manage members, fines and reminders | Set borrowing rules per membership type, collect fines, and email reminders.
## Members and borrowing rules

Create **membership types** with rules: how many items, for how many days, how many renewals, the **fine per day** and reservations. Members have an expiry date, and you can **suspend** one.

## Fines

Fines for **overdue** and **lost** items are created automatically. Record **payments** or **waive** a fine. You can also **post fines to Finance** as a draft journal entry.

## Email reminders

Send **due soon**, **overdue** and **ready for pickup** emails. A member gets one email per kind. Reminders are **off until you switch them on**.

@@ member-portal | How the member portal works | Give members a public page to sign in, renew, search, reserve and see their fines.
Each library can have a **public member portal**.

1. A library administrator opens the portal. It is **off until the library opens it**.
2. A member signs in with a **card number and PIN**.
3. They can see their loans, **renew**, **search** the catalogue, **reserve** or cancel a reservation, and see their **fines**.

The portal is in English only today. Members cannot self-register or pay fines online yet.
