@@ run-an-audit-and-read-the-health-score | How to run an audit and read the health score | Crawl up to 500 pages against more than 30 rules and get a 0 to 100 health score.
## What an audit checks

An audit crawls **up to 500 pages** and checks **more than 30 rules**: titles, descriptions, headings, canonical tags, structured data, images and alt text, speed, redirects, broken links, duplicates, orphan pages, sitemap and robots.

## The health score

You get a **0 to 100 health score** and a history of audits. Issues are remembered, so a re-run updates them and closes the ones you fixed.

## Cost

An audit costs **1 credit per 25 pages crawled**. You see the cost before you run it. See [Credits for SEO](/docs/seo/credits-for-seo).

@@ review-and-approve-fixes | How to review and approve fixes | The agent proposes fixes for new issues. Nothing changes until you approve it.
## Fixes you approve

For new issues, the agent **proposes fixes**. Review each one. **Nothing changes until you approve it.**

## A limit to know about

On websites that are **not hosted on Vrodux**, approved fixes are applied by a script in the visitor's browser, which search crawlers and social previews may not see. WordPress articles can be pushed, but existing WordPress pages are not edited at source.

@@ competitor-analysis-and-content-plan | How to analyse competitors and plan content | Add up to five competitors, see the topics they cover that you do not, and turn the gaps into a 30-day plan.
## Competitor analysis

Add up to **five competitor websites** and a **search market**. You see topics they cover that you do not and, **where keyword data is available**, search terms they rank for that you do not.

## 30-day content plan

The gaps become a **calendar of article ideas**. Write a draft with one click into a **review queue**. Publishing to WordPress is a **separate step you choose**.

## Not included

A keyword research tool, rank tracking, backlink analysis and AI-search visibility are not available. A bigger keyword and ranking toolset is planned.
