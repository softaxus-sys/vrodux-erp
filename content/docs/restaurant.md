@@ floors-tables-and-menu | How to set up floors, tables and your menu | Design your dining floors and tables, and build or import a menu.
## Floors and tables

Create your **floors** and **dining areas**, add **tables**, and arrange them in the **floor designer** so the screen matches your room.

## Build the menu

- Add **categories** and **dishes** with photos.
- Use the **availability toggle** to take a dish off when it runs out.
- Add **modifiers**: priced extras that can be required or optional, with a minimum and maximum.

## Import a menu

**Bulk import** a menu from Excel or CSV, with photos by URL. A 62-dish sample menu is included if you want to try the system with demo data.

@@ take-orders-with-modifiers | How to take dine-in, takeaway and delivery orders | Use the order screen with modifiers, special instructions, hold and recall.
1. Open the **order screen** and choose **dine-in**, **takeaway** or **delivery**.
2. For dine-in, pick the table.
3. Choose dishes by category. Add **modifiers** (required ones must be filled) and **special instructions**.
4. Send the order to the kitchen. Use **hold and recall** to keep an order open and come back to it.

Delivery orders are punched on the same order screen. Orders attach to the cashier's open shift, so waiters do not need their own till.

@@ kitchen-display | How to use the kitchen display | See tickets on the kitchen screen and mark them ready and served.
The **Kitchen Display** shows each order as a ticket.

1. A new order appears on the kitchen screen.
2. The kitchen marks the ticket **ready**.
3. Service marks it **served**.

Kitchen configuration lets you set the screen up for your stations. Users with the Kitchen Staff role see the kitchen tools.

@@ bills-split-payments-and-tips | How to bill, split and take payment | Split a bill by item, add tips and discounts, and refund or void with an audit trail.
## Settle a bill

1. Open the bill from the order.
2. Apply a **discount**, which needs a reason.
3. Add a **tip** if the guest leaves one.
4. Take payment, using more than one payment method if needed.
5. Print the **tax receipt**.

## Split the bill

Use **split bills by item** to give each guest their own items. Whole lines can be split; splitting part of a line quantity is not available.

## Refunds and voids

Refunds and voids need a reason, and every discount, void and refund is **audited**. Each is controlled by its own permission.

## Customers and loyalty

Link a customer to a bill. They earn points at **1 point per 100 spent** and can redeem them as a discount. Wallet and house-account payments are also supported.

@@ delivery-and-riders | How to run delivery with riders | Dispatch orders and give riders a mobile screen with navigation and amount to collect.
1. Punch a **delivery** order on the order screen.
2. Open the **dispatcher board** and assign it to a rider.
3. The rider opens the mobile-friendly **rider screen**, which shows the address with navigation, a call button and the **amount to collect**.

Riders are linked to their user logins, and there is a Delivery Rider role. There are also **tracking links** for customers. A rider marking an order delivered does not change the kitchen order status.

@@ restaurant-roles-and-reports | Restaurant roles, shifts and reports | The roles to give your team, how shifts work, and the dashboards.
## Roles

The restaurant comes with these roles: **Restaurant Manager**, **Staff**, **Cashier**, **Waiter**, **Kitchen Staff** and **Delivery Rider**. Permissions cover tables, menu, orders (void, discount, refund), kitchen, reservations, delivery and reports.

## Shifts

Orders attach to the cashier's open shift so payments land in the Z-report. See [How to open and close a shift](/docs/pos/open-and-close-a-shift).

## Reports and QR

The restaurant has reports and dashboards. QR table ordering and tracking links are available. Restaurant orders and refunds can also be posted to the ledger through the Finance Integration Center.
