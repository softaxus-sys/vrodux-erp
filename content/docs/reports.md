@@ run-and-export-reports | How to run and export a report | Find a report in the hub, filter it, and export it to CSV or PDF.
1. Open the **Reports hub**. It shows only the modules you have and can access.
2. Choose a module. The counts today are **POS 19**, **Inventory 17**, **CRM 8**, **Library 12** and **Import & Export 8**.
3. Open a report and **filter** it by date, owner and other fields.
4. **Export** the result to CSV or PDF.

## Good to know

- Results respect each person's access. A rep only sees their own records.
- Vrodux also has dashboards on each module's home screen. See [Your Dashboard](/docs/start-here/your-dashboard).
