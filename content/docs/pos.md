@@ open-and-close-a-shift | How to open and close a shift | Open a shift with a float, record cash in and out, and close with a count and a Z-report.
## Open a shift

1. On the cashier screen, choose to open a shift and enter the starting cash float.
2. Start selling. Sales, refunds and cash movements are attached to your shift.

## During the day

- Record **cash in** and **cash out** with a category, for example paying a supplier from the till.
- A supervisor can approve sessions where your role requires it.

## Close the shift

1. Choose to close the shift.
2. **Count the cash** and enter it.
3. Vrodux compares the count with what it expected and shows the difference.
4. Print the **Z-report**.

Restaurant orders attach to the cashier's open shift too, so their payments land in the same Z-report. See [Retail & Restaurant POS](/docs/pos/retail-and-restaurant).

@@ sell-refund-and-void-with-the-right-permissions | How to sell, refund and void | Use barcode scanning, hold and recall, and control who can refund, void or discount.
## Make a sale

- Scan a **barcode** or search for the product.
- Use **hold and recall** to park a sale while the customer fetches something.
- Apply a discount if your role allows it, and take payment.

## Refunds, voids and discounts

Each of these has its **own permission**, so a cashier role cannot refund unless you grant it. Voiding a sale returns stock correctly, for both inventory-based and POS-based products.

## Customers

The customer book keeps customers, **wallet or store credit**, **loyalty** and **vouchers**.

## Permission tiers

Roles come in cashier, supervisor and manager tiers. You set permissions for sales, refund, void, discount, sessions, products, customers and reports.

@@ customer-credit-accounts | How to sell on credit and collect payment | Give customers a house account with a limit and terms, and track ageing and reminders.
## Set up a credit account

For a customer, switch on **buy on credit** and set a **credit limit** and **payment terms**. Each credit sale gets a due date.

## Collect

- Record payments against the account.
- The customer's **statement PDF** shows what is outstanding.
- Open the **Credit Control** page to see all accounts. Balances are grouped into **ageing buckets**: not due, 1–30, 31–60, 61–90 and 90+ days.
- **Stop credit** for a customer who is overdue.

## Reminders

- Send a **WhatsApp reminder link** to the customer.
- Turn on **daily email reminders**. They are off until you switch them on.

@@ offline-mode-and-sync | How offline mode works | Keep selling without internet and upload everything with Sync to Cloud at day end.
## Turn it on

Offline mode is a **per-workspace switch**. It works in the browser and in the Windows desktop app.

## While offline

Tills record **shifts, sales, refunds, voids and cash movements** locally. Offline sales are never rejected for stock; if stock goes negative, it is flagged.

## When you are back online

Choose **Sync to Cloud** at day end. Syncing twice is safe.

## Good to know

- Switching modes requires everything to be synced first.
- Vouchers, loyalty and customer search are not available offline, and adding a product needs a connection.
- Voiding a sale made before going offline cannot be done while offline.

@@ pos-dashboard-and-reports | How to read the POS dashboard and reports | See sales, refunds, voids, payment mix and top products, and run the 19 POS reports.
## The dashboard

The POS dashboard shows **gross and net sales**, **refunds**, **voids**, the **trend against the previous period**, the **payment mix**, **top products and cashiers**, and **open shifts**.

## Reports

There are **19 POS reports** in the Reports hub. Open the Reports hub, choose Point of Sale, pick a report, and export it as CSV or PDF. See [Reports hub](/docs/reports/overview).

## Finance

POS sales, refunds, wallet top-ups and cash movements can be sent to the ledger through the Finance Integration Center. See [How the Integration Center posts to the ledger](/docs/finance/integration-center-and-finance-posting).
