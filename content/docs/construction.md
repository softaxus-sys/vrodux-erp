The Construction module is built for construction businesses. It tracks **construction projects**, **bills of quantities** and the **contractors** working on them.

## Projects

Create a project and keep its details in one place. Follow the progress of the site or project.

@@ construction-costs-and-connected-modules | Project costs and connected modules | See how project costs and contractor payments flow through Purchase, HR and Finance.
Construction works **with** other modules instead of tracking costs separately. **Project costs and contractor payments flow through Purchase, HR and Finance.**

- Use [Purchase](/docs/purchase) for what you buy for a project.
- Use [HR](/docs/hr) for the people on it.
- Use [Finance](/docs/finance) for payments and the ledger.

Read about [construction cost tracking](/blog/construction-erp-cost-tracking) for why this matters.
