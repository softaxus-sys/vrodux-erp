@@ list-properties-and-import-stock | How to list properties and import your stock | Create a property and unit in one step, filter your stock, and import sheets from Excel or CSV.
## One listing, one unit

A **listing** is a property and a unit together. You create the building as you add the unit, and a same-named building is reused. Property types are free text. Listings cover both **rental and sale** stock, with purpose, listed date, bedrooms, price or rent, area, media, and owner and agent details.

## Filter your stock

Use the multi-select filters for building, bedrooms, type, status, furnishing, city, agent and price range. Active filters show as removable chips.

## Import

Import rental or sale stock sheets from Excel or CSV. Sale prices go to **asking price** (not rent), and occupancy is read from the price cell. The original sheet cells are kept next to the parsed figures.

## Publish

Each property has a profile with photos that you can **print** or **publish on your website**.

@@ leases-and-rent-schedules | How to create a lease and collect rent | Create a lease for a vacant unit, generate the rent schedule, record payments and waive instalments.
1. Create a **lease** with a tenant and a **vacant unit**.
2. Choose the **payment frequency**: monthly, quarterly, semi-annual or annual.
3. Vrodux generates the **rent schedule**, and the instalments always add up exactly.
4. **Record payments** against instalments as they arrive.
5. **Waive** an instalment if you agree to.
6. Take **advance rent at signing**. It is applied across the instalments in order.

Rent can be posted to Finance through the Integration Center.

@@ rent-reminders-and-vacancy-alerts | How to set up rent reminders and alerts | Send tenants due-soon, overdue and lease-expiry emails, and get alerts when a unit is about to fall vacant.
## Email reminders

Set up reminders for:

- **rent due soon**, for example 30, 7 and 1 days before
- **overdue rent**, with chasers that repeat up to a cap
- **lease expiry notices** at 90, 60 and 30 days

Add a CC list, set your time zone, **preview** a message, and use **run now** to send. Each notice is sent **once**, and reminders stop when rent is paid.

Reminders are **off until you configure them**, and Vrodux tells you plainly if no mail account is set.

## Vacancy alerts

"About to fall vacant" alerts are in-app notifications to the unit's agent and your administrators. They are not emails.

@@ renew-or-terminate-a-lease | How to renew or terminate a lease | Renew with a new schedule and carried-over arrears, or terminate with a final account.
## Renew

1. Open the lease and choose **Renew**.
2. The new lease **links to the old one**, gets a new schedule, and any **unpaid rent carries over**.

## Terminate

1. Open the lease and choose **Terminate**.
2. Enter the **date** and **reason**. A later date acts as notice.
3. Vrodux works out the **final account**: deposit plus paid, minus owed, minus penalty, minus deductions.
4. **Settle the refund**.

A "Mark expired" button exists for leases past their end date. Leases do not expire automatically.
