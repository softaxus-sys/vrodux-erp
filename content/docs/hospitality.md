@@ front-desk-check-in-and-out | How to use the front desk | Handle arrivals, check-in, check-out, cancellations, no-shows and room moves.
The **Front Desk** screen lists **arrivals**, **in-house** guests and **departures**.

## Check in

1. Open an arrival and choose check-in.
2. Optionally **change the room**.
3. Confirm.

## Check out

Check-out is blocked while the guest has an unpaid balance, unless you allow it.

## Other changes

- **No-show** a booking that does not arrive.
- **Cancel** with a reason and a fee.
- **Amend dates**, which re-prices the stay.
- **Move room** during a stay.

The **Room Calendar (tape chart)** and the **Reservations** list show all bookings.

@@ rooms-room-types-and-rates | How to set up rooms, room types and rates | Create rooms and room types, block rooms, and set seasonal rates and rate plans.
## Rooms

Add your **rooms** and **room types**. Use **out-of-order blocks** to take a room out of availability. Availability respects blocks and cancelled stays.

## Rate plans

Create **rate plans** with meal plan and refundable options. A default plan called "BAR" exists. Set **seasonal weekday and weekend rates** per room type, and a **minimum stay**. A booking keeps the rates it was sold at.

## Guests

Guest profiles are kept across stays, and a returning guest is found by ID, email or phone.

## Hotel settings

Set the **check-in and check-out times**, **weekend days**, **service charge %** and **tax %**.

@@ folios-and-housekeeping | How to manage folios and housekeeping | Post charges and payments to a guest folio, print the bill, and let housekeeping tasks create themselves.
## The folio

A guest's **folio** holds room charges, extras, payments and any discount. You can **void** a charge. Totals follow the GCC style, with **service charge %**, **tax %** and an optional fixed nightly fee. Print the bill when the guest checks out.

## Housekeeping

**Housekeeping tasks** are created automatically when a guest checks out. Staff update them as rooms are cleaned.

## Finance

Hotel payments can be posted to Finance through the Integration Center. See [How the Integration Center posts to the ledger](/docs/finance/integration-center-and-finance-posting).

@@ tour-packages-departures-and-bookings | How to sell tour packages and take bookings | Create packages, schedule departures with seats, and book travellers with instalments.
## Create a package

Add a **tour package** with its duration, **price per adult and per child**, a **single-room supplement**, and mark it active or inactive.

## Schedule departures

Add **departures**: dates with a number of **seats** and per-departure prices. The screen shows **seats left** and whether a departure is open or closed.

## Take a booking

1. Choose a departure (dates and prices come from it), or create a **custom-dated trip**.
2. Enter adults, children, single rooms, any price override, discount, tax and the booking source.
3. Confirm the booking now, or **save it as an enquiry**.

Vrodux **refuses more travellers than seats left**. Add traveller details from the booking after it is created.

## Payments

Add **services**, **instalments** and **payments** to a booking, and print documents. The Tourism dashboard shows packages and bookings.
